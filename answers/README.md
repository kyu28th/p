# Answers

Answer files follow a three-stage lifecycle.

## `drafts/`

Initial answer written under exam-like constraints.

## `reviewed/`

Answer after AI or human review, including score, missing keywords, structure issues, and weak-point tags.

## `model/`

Refined answer for memorization and review.

## Weak-Point Flow

Repeated issues found in reviewed answers should be reflected in `study/weak-points.md`.

Common tags:

- weak-definition
- weak-structure
- missing-keyword
- weak-comparison
- weak-diagram
- weak-case
- weak-conclusion
- time-over
