## Learning Content

- WBS is a work breakdown structure for decomposing the full project scope into manageable units.
- Work Package is the lowest manageable unit in WBS.
- 100% Rule means child work must cover 100% of the parent scope, avoiding both omission and over-inclusion.
- WBS is used as a basis for schedule, cost, assignment, risk, and communication management.
